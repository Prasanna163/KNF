# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the NCIForge community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
career stage.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, constructive, scientifically honest, and considerate of other
contributors. Discuss evidence and implementation choices without personal
attacks. Clearly distinguish validated results from hypotheses, exploratory
work, and software defects.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of
private information, and knowingly misleading scientific claims are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that
violate this policy. Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy
and be proportionate to the behaviour.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
